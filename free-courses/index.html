AI for Work & Family
Your First Augmented Life

A practical introduction to using AI where real life gets complicated: work, family, information, recurring tasks, decisions and time.

10 lessons · beginner · self-paced · free

What this course is for

You do not need another tour of AI tools. This course helps you identify where AI can remove friction from your actual life, where it should not be used, and how to turn one useful idea into a repeatable workflow.

01

What AI augmentation means

Separate useful augmentation from hype. Identify tasks where AI can extend your capability without replacing your judgement.

02

Your personal AI environment

Map the information, tools, people and recurring responsibilities that make up your working and family life.

03

Reduce repetitive cognitive load

Find the drafting, summarising, organising and preparation work that can be delegated safely.

04

Family coordination

Use AI to prepare schedules, compare information and coordinate competing commitments without losing human control.

05

Assistants vs agents

Understand the difference between asking AI for help and delegating a bounded workflow to an agent.

06

Prompting and delegation

Give AI the context, constraints, desired output and evidence it needs to produce useful work.

07

Build your first workflow

Turn one recurring pain point into a small, testable AI workflow you can actually use.

08

Privacy, consent and boundaries

Decide what should stay human, what information needs protection, and where consent matters.

09

Human judgement

Learn where AI can prepare, compare and explain—and where the decision remains yours.

10

Your 30-day augmentation plan

Choose a small number of high-value experiments and turn them into a practical next month.

What you leave with

An AI opportunity mapA clearer view of where AI may save time or reduce friction.
One working workflowA concrete task converted into a repeatable AI-assisted process.
A 30-day planA small sequence of experiments rather than an overwhelming tool list.
